# Think Bigger

Think Bigger explores opportunities for AI-assisted contributions to human knowledge and well-being.

## Language

**Candidate problem**:
A question or problem, curated from existing work or developed here, being considered for an AI-assisted investigation.

**Investigation**:
An effort to make progress on a candidate problem and gather evidence about its results.

**Operator**:
The person directing research or an investigation, who may be unfamiliar with the problem's domain.

**Success test**:
A method for assessing whether a proposed result meets a problem's objective, which may be deterministic, statistical, or learned.

**Contribution**:
A new result that advances knowledge or human well-being and is supported by credible evidence.

## Relationships

- A **Candidate problem** may lead to multiple **Investigations** over time.
- An **Operator** works with agents to discover candidate problems and direct investigations.
- An **Investigation** may use or develop a **Success test**; the test's reliability is itself a question to assess.
- An **Investigation** may yield a **Contribution**, but an unsuccessful attempt can also produce useful findings.

## Example dialogue

> **Operator:** "I don't know this field yet. Can we understand this candidate problem well enough to plan an investigation?"
> **Agent:** "We can examine its background and possible success tests, including what evidence would establish that those tests are useful."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Harness" remains exploratory: shared research support and a general problem-solving system have not been established as the same thing.
